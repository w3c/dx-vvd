### Recap of last meeting/Scope of the work of the task force

Dan\> Variable Cascade a very good basis to begin with.

Arofan\> I see 4 important inputs: (1) The variable cascade (lots of implementations, DDI, GSIM, CDIF); (2) Observable Properties (SOSA/SSN, etc.); (3) DataCube. (4) iADOPT.

Rob\> Observable Properties : identifiable object that can be measured. Not modelled by SOSA/SSN \- assumes a dereferenceable identifier. Whatever model we support needs to support federation where sets of variable descriptions are shared and reused.

Arofan\> The work should encompass bot structures and the variable cascade, because we will need to assign roles to variables (“components” in DDI-CDI and GSIM) and although these are not always needed, they will provide a useful framework in some cases (DataCube, etc.) I would argue that we should look at structures from the start.

Arofan\> For the existing model and syntax bindings of the variable cascade in RDF, look at [https\://docs.ddialliance.org/DDI-CDI/1.0/model/FieldLevelDocumentation/DDICDILibrary/Classes/Conceptual/InstanceVariable.html\#super-class-hierarchy-generalization](https://docs.ddialliance.org/DDI-CDI/1.0/model/FieldLevelDocumentation/DDICDILibrary/Classes/Conceptual/InstanceVariable.html#super-class-hierarchy-generalization)  including the tabs at the bottom showing syntax representations  

Rob\> Also interested in the scope Arofan suggested. Specifically interested in:

* how the model supports federated registers of descriptions.  
* support for canonical structural implementation in JSON-LD (but supporting other structure frameworks as required)  
* mapping to related models \- for both explanation and (critically) testing  
* modular specification construction \- how to, for example, combine VVD and DCAT to create profiles \- without duplicating hand-written content \- i.e. driving spec generation from the machine readable artefacts.  
* potential co-publication and sharing of tooling/methodology via the W3C/OGC Spatial Data on the Web WG (and can we do something with DDI and CDIF communities) \- this will support combination and re-use   
* in general support all aspects of the FAIR principles \- but testing Reuse patterns as the key discipline

Arofan\> It is suggested that we will want to combine DCAT and VVD, I see the description of the variables in a DCAT entry as a primary use case for us.
